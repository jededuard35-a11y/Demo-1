What we serve

Coffee, plates, and a chair in the shade

Three things bring people back to the Granary: the grinder, the kitchen, and the garden tables where nobody rushes you out.

Specialty Coffee

Espresso, pour-over, and cold brew, pulled to order. Ask what is on the grinder this week and we will happily talk your ear off about it.

Chef-Crafted Meals

Plates made in-house from what the market had that morning. All-day breakfast, rice bowls, pasta, and a few things built for sharing.

Pet-Friendly Outdoor Seating

Shaded tables on the garden side where leashed dogs are welcome. Water bowls come out before you even have to ask for them.

Order at the counter, then take a seat inside or out. The garden tables are first come, first served.

About the café

A slow corner of the Granary grounds

The Lounge sits inside The Granary in Cabetican, just off Bacolor River Control Road. The bar faces the courtyard, the kitchen runs behind it, and the garden tables sit under the trees where the river air actually reaches you.

There is no rush here. Order at the counter, pick a spot, and stay for the second cup. Bring the dog, bring a book, or bring the group chat that can never decide where to eat.

  • Bar seats inside, shaded tables out in the garden
  • Good for solo coffees, group lunches, and leashed dogs
  • A short drive from Bacolor town center, right off the river road
