About the café

A Café Built Around the Granary

The Lounge Café at the Granary is a neighborhood café in Cabetican, Bacolor. You can meet friends over coffee, order a full meal, or take a quiet table outside with your pet.

The kitchen focuses on chef-crafted plates that pair well with coffee and conversation. Whether you are stopping by after errands or settling in for the afternoon, the café keeps the setting relaxed and the service direct.
